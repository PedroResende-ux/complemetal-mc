# Complemetal brand assets

The primary mark is a single geometric `C` made from brushed graphite and
silver metal with one restrained cyan inset. It is designed to remain legible
at Minecraft and Modrinth icon sizes without text, glow clouds, floating
particles or decorative background elements.

- `complemetal-icon-source.png`: 1254 x 1254 transparent master image.
- `src/main/resources/assets/complemetal/icon.png`: 256 x 256 transparent
  release icon, 55,844 bytes.

Generation mode: image generation, transparent raster master. Final prompt:

> Minimalist premium app icon / mod logo for “Complemetal”. A single bold
> geometric letter C constructed from two interlocking machined-metal bands,
> brushed graphite and satin silver, with one very restrained thin cyan inset
> line suggesting a render pipeline. Nearly flat orthographic construction,
> crisp silhouette, subtle physically plausible bevels, balanced negative
> space, centered and readable at 32 px. Transparent background. No words, no
> extra symbols, no circuit-board pattern, no sparks, no particles, no glow
> cloud, no dramatic scene, no generic AI concept-art aesthetic.

The generated master was manually selected for the simplified release mark
and downscaled to the packaged 256 px asset. Keep the transparent master when
exporting future store or social variants.
